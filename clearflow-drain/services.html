02 / Deep cleaning

Hydro jetting

Mechanical clearing opens a path. Hydro jetting goes further, directing controlled high-pressure water through the line to scour grease, sludge, mineral scale, and fine roots from the entire pipe wall.

  • Restores more of the pipe’s working diameter
  • Effective for recurring grease and sludge buildup
  • Pressure matched to line material and condition

Ask ClearFlow
How do I know if the main sewer line is blocked?

Multiple fixtures backing up together, water appearing in a shower when a toilet flushes, or gurgling throughout the home can point to a main-line blockage. Stop running water and call for an assessment.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

It can be, but the line should be evaluated first. We consider pipe material, age, and condition, often using a camera inspection, then match pressure and nozzles to the system.

How often should a grease trap be cleaned?

Frequency depends on trap size, kitchen volume, menu, and local rules. Many kitchens need service every one to three months. We can assess accumulation and recommend a practical interval.

Will I receive a price before work starts?

Yes. After the initial assessment, your technician explains the recommended work and provides an upfront estimate. Additional work is never performed without your approval.

Do you service both homes and businesses?

Yes. Our crews work in homes, restaurants, retail spaces, offices, multi-unit properties, and light industrial facilities throughout the ClearFlow service area.
