Common questions

A few things worth knowing early.

How do I know whether I need repair or replacement?

It depends on the age, extent, location, and underlying cause of the damage. A site review is the best way to compare a focused repair with a larger replacement scope.

Can an existing deck frame be reused?

Sometimes. We evaluate the frame, footings, connections, dimensions, and current condition before recommending resurfacing or a full rebuild.

What should I have ready for an estimate?

Your address, a short description of the issue or goal, a few photos if available, and any timing concerns are enough to begin.

What happens when weather interrupts the schedule?

Exterior work depends on safe, workable conditions. We plan around the forecast, communicate changes, and keep exposed areas protected during the project.
